# CU1 - the post-0.1.0-pre.2 cleanup - what the first repo-wide
over-engineering audit found

## 1. Question and bounded scope

> After release 0.1.0-pre.2, which code in the product package can be cut,
> merged, or replaced by the platform, without changing any behavior the
> correctness contract protects?

This note records the findings of one repo-wide over-engineering audit. It
ranks each finding by the size of its cut. It names the replacement for every
cut. It applies nothing. It changes no production code.

The audit scope is complexity only. Correctness defects, security holes, and
performance are out of scope. A finding that touches the correctness contract
is marked as a decision, not as a cut.

Labels: `[SOURCE]` is a fact read in the tree. `[MEASURED]` is a fact produced
by a run in this audit. `[INFERENCE]` is a conclusion or an estimate.
`[DECISION NEEDED]` is a choice the controller or the user must make.

are no speculative dependencies. The package declares only NDMF.

frontends share most of their text, and the semantics layer produces two
output channels that nothing reads. Together they hold roughly 1,400 of the

## 4. Findings, biggest cut first

### 4.1 CU1 - the two lilToon mode frontends share most of their text

`Editor/Semantics/LilToon/LilToonCutoutMaterialSemantics.cs` holds 754 lines.
`Editor/Semantics/LilToon/LilToonTransparentMaterialSemantics.cs` holds 833
lines. `[MEASURED]`

The audit normalized both files by replacing the mode words and the
`LIL_RENDER` digit, then diffed them. Only 65 lines of the cutout file and 144
lines of the transparent file differ from the other file. `[MEASURED]` About
690 lines are shared text. This covers the request tables, the layer walks,
and the gate checks. The identical `_Main2ndTex`, `_Main3rdTex`, and blend
mask request blocks appear in both files. `[SOURCE]`

Replacement: one mode-parameterized core inside the lilToon family. Each
frontend keeps its theorem, its eligibility gates, and its refusal vocabulary.
The shared request table and the shared evidence walk move to one place.

`[DECISION NEEDED]` The repository policy says shader-specific behavior stays
shader-specific. These two files are two render modes of one attested shader,
not two shaders. The controller must confirm that a shared core inside the
lilToon family is inside the policy. The design record in
`LilToonOpaqueConversionResult.cs:109-115` already draws this line for the
eligibility modules. It keeps constants shared and evaluation mode-specific.

### 4.2 CU2 - the semantics layer produces two output channels that nothing reads

`MaterialSemantics` carries `Emission` and `Normal` outputs
(`Editor/Semantics/MaterialSemantics.cs:1182-1183`). Both frontends populate
them. No production code reads either property. `[SOURCE]` The audit grepped
every read of `.Emission` and `.Normal` across the product and research
packages. All readers are the four semantics test files. The analysis and
build modules never consult these channels.

The producer-only track holds:

| Piece | Location | Lines |
|---|---|---|
| LilToon emission interpretation | `LilToon/LilToonMaterialSemantics.cs:600-880` | about 280 |
| LilToon normal interpretation | `LilToon/LilToonMaterialSemantics.cs:881-985` | about 105 |
| Poiyomi emission interpretation | `Poiyomi/PoiyomiMaterialSemantics.cs:1066-1257` | about 190 |
| Poiyomi normal interpretation | `Poiyomi/PoiyomiMaterialSemantics.cs:1258-1355` | about 98 |
| `NormalSemanticValue` type | `Editor/Semantics/MaterialSemantics.cs:1048-1118` | 71 |
| Output plumbing | constructor arms in both frontends | about 15 |

The paired tests hold 1,510 more lines. `[MEASURED]` They are
`LilToonNormalTests` at 267, `LilToonEmissionTests` at 440,
`PoiyomiNormalTests` at 298, and `PoiyomiEmissionTests` at 505.

Replacement: nothing. Restore the channels when a consumer lands.

`[DECISION NEEDED]` The architecture vision describes broad material
understanding as the long-term direction. These channels may be staged work
toward that direction. The controller must decide between cutting now and
re-adding under a consumer, or keeping them as staged output. The horizon
record of 2026-09-04 already treats understanding without a consumer as a
coverage gap, not a product.

### 4.3 CU3 - the cutout specification layer has no production caller

`Editor/Semantics/ToonMaterialCutoutSemantics.cs` (95 lines) and
`Editor/Semantics/MaterialCutoutSpecification.cs` (37 lines) define
`EvaluateCutoutSpecification` and its result type. `[SOURCE]` An exhaustive
grep finds no production caller. The only references are the two files
themselves and `Tests/Editor/Semantics/ToonMaterialCutoutSemanticsTests.cs`
(98 lines). `[MEASURED]`

The cutoff now travels through the texture evidence request. A frontend
declares its cutoff scalar in `TexturePropertyEvidenceRequest`, and the
capture routes binarize by it
(`Editor/Host/UnityMaterialEvidenceCapture.cs:1100-1104`). `[SOURCE]` The
layer was superseded.

Replacement: nothing. Delete both files and the test file.

### 4.4 CU4 - `ScalarSemanticValue` carries two encodings of one product

`ScalarSemanticValueKind` has both `ProductOfTextureSamples` and
`ProductChainOfTextureSamples`
(`Editor/Semantics/MaterialSemantics.cs:445-454`). The chain kind accepts two
or more factors. Its own doc states that two factors are the historical
product shape (`:678-686`). `[SOURCE]` So the pair kind is a strict special
case of the chain kind.

The pair encoding costs one kind, one factory (`:577-595`), five accessors
(`:629-676`), one guard (`:919-927`), one dual-kind accessor check
(`:665-676`), and two equality or hashing arms (`:947-952`, `:999-1004`).

Replacement: `ProductChain` with two factors. Callers of the five accessors
move to the chain accessors. Saves about 120 lines.

### 4.5 CU5 - the build operation layer has one production caller

`Editor/Build/AmuseBuildOperation.cs` (160 lines) defines two delegates, a
decision struct, a result class, an outcome enum, and one `Execute` method.
The only production caller is `AlphaSeparationApply.cs:38`. `[SOURCE]` The
delegate seams exist so tests can drive the boundary.

Replacement: inline the gate, prepare, and apply sequence into
`AlphaSeparationApply.Execute`. Fold the outcome assertions of
`AmuseBuildOperationTests` (649 lines) into the apply tests. Saves about 130
production lines and shrinks one test file.

### 4.6 CU6 - the analysis sampling enums mirror the semantics sampling enums

`TriangleAlphaClassifier.cs:15-46` defines `AlphaFilterMode`,
`AlphaWrapMode`, and `AlphaAnisoMode`. They mirror `TextureFilterMode`,
`TextureWrapMode`, and `TextureAnisoMode` in
`Editor/Semantics/MaterialSemantics.cs:102-132`, member for member.
`AlphaSemanticsResolver.cs:850-900` translates between them with three
identity switches. `[SOURCE]` The translation is exhaustive on purpose. The
doc at `:844-849` records that intent.

The analysis module already depends on the semantics value algebra. `AffineUvTransform.cs`
imports `Alrauna.Amuse.Editor.Semantics` at line 4. `[SOURCE]` So the mirror
is not a layering requirement.

Replacement: the classifier consumes the semantics enums directly. Delete the
three mirror enums and the three switches. Saves about 75 lines. The fail-closed
default arm moves to the semantics constructors, which already validate their
enum inputs.

### 4.7 CU7 - 36 hand-written equality members

The product tree holds 36 `Equals` and `GetHashCode` members across 11
hash-code sites. `[MEASURED]` The value classes of the semantics and host
layers write them by hand. `ScalarSemanticValue` alone spends 93 lines on
equality (`Editor/Semantics/MaterialSemantics.cs:929-1021`). `[SOURCE]`

Replacement: C# 9 non-positional records. A `sealed record` with private
fields synthesizes member equality, hashing, and operators without any
`init` accessor. Unity 2022.3 compiles C# 9. `[INFERENCE]` A compile probe
must confirm this before the change. Saves 300 to 400 lines.

### 4.8 CU8 - one wrapper only delegates

`PoiyomiMaterialSemantics.TryGetColorInterpretation`
(`Poiyomi/PoiyomiMaterialSemantics.cs:1837-1843`) forwards to
`UnityTextureEvidence.TryGetColorInterpretation` and nothing else. `[SOURCE]`
Three test call sites use the wrapper
(`Tests/Editor/Semantics/Poiyomi/PoiyomiTextureEvidenceTests.cs:460,474,489`).

Replacement: direct calls to `UnityTextureEvidence`. Saves 7 lines.

### 4.9 CU9 - three near-identical block-state scanners

`AdmittedMaterialStates.cs:876-945` holds `TryGetBlockFloat`,
`TryGetBlockColor`, and `TryGetBlockVector`. Each scans the same entry list
and differs only in the accepted `ShaderPropertyType` and the out type.
`[SOURCE]`

Replacement: one typed lookup keyed on `ShaderPropertyType`. Saves about 30
lines.

### 4.10 CU10 - a hand-rolled `Contains`

`AdmittedMaterialStates.cs:946-964` implements `ContainsName` over 19 lines.
`[SOURCE]`

Replacement: `names.Contains(targetName, StringComparer.Ordinal)` from
System.Linq. Saves 18 lines. The call is build-time editor code. Allocation
cost is not a constraint here.

### 4.11 CU11 - a public class that wraps one boolean expression

`Runtime/AmuseComponentPlacement.cs` (19 lines) exposes
`IsOnHierarchyRoot`, which returns `component.transform.parent == null`. The
only caller is the inspector help box
(`Editor/AmuseAvatarOptimizerEditor.cs:24`). `[SOURCE]` The build gate
re-checks placement against the NDMF avatar root on its own. The paired test
file holds 32 lines. `[MEASURED]`

Replacement: the comparison inline in the inspector. Delete the class and
the test file. Saves 19 production lines and 32 test lines.

## 5. Checked and not flagged

These were examined and kept. Each entry names the reason.

- The three texture evidence routes. The GPU blit route, the streaming clone
  route, and the disk decode route each document a distinct measured failure
  mode. They are not parallel duplicates. `Editor/Host/`.
- The attestation tables. `LilToonSourceAttestation.cs` and the Poiyomi pins
  are policy-mandated measured data. Size is the cost of the policy.
- `BaseMaterialSemanticsProvider` in `UnityRendererAlphaAnalysis.cs:187`. The
  census collector is a live consumer. The temporary note is accurate.
- `VersionConsentDialog` and its presenter seam. The seam drives the batch
  and decline test paths without Unity UI calls.
- `CensusGuard`, the census record stack, and the research tests. Each has a
  live consumer or is the research purpose itself.
- `CensusReflection.ReferenceEqualityComparer`. The .NET Standard 2.1 profile
  of Unity 2022.3 does not ship the BCL type. The hand-rolled comparer is
  required.
- The `TextureEvidenceKinds` flag set. Every flag is requested by at least
  one frontend. `[SOURCE]`
- The serialized fields of `AmuseAvatarOptimizer`. Every field has an
  inspector control and a build consumer. `[SOURCE]`
- Dependencies. The product declares only NDMF. Nothing is removable.

## 6. Recommended order of work

This record authorizes nothing. It orders the findings by risk so a plan can
consume them.

1. CU3, CU8, CU10, CU11 first. Each is a pure deletion with no policy
   question and no contract contact.
2. CU4, CU5, CU6, CU9 next. Each is a local reshape with full test coverage
   already in place.
3. CU7 after a compile probe confirms record support on Unity 2022.3.
4. CU1 and CU2 last. Both need the policy decision recorded in their
   sections. CU1 changes the shape of the two largest semantics files. CU2
   removes staged output that the architecture vision may want soon.

Total if all findings land: about 2,300 production lines and about 3,900
lines with the paired tests. `[INFERENCE]` No dependency changes.
